Quick answers to common questions. For step-by-step guidance, follow the links to the full articles.

## Bid matching

### How quickly does a new profile start producing matches?

Matching runs once a day, after GPH imports new solicitations. A profile you create today is evaluated on the next run — usually within 24 hours. See [How Solicitation Matching Works](/help/solicitation-matching).

### Can I match against solicitations posted before I signed up?

No. Bid matching evaluates newly imported solicitations on each run, not the existing backlog. To research solicitations that are already posted, use the [Searching for Parts](/help/parts-search) and [Researching Vendors](/help/vendor-research) tools, which surface recent and open solicitations tied to a part or vendor.

### Why does one run date show solicitations posted on other days?

The run date is when GPH found the match; the **Posted** date is when the government put the solicitation on the street. They usually differ. A day's harvest routinely brings in solicitations that were posted earlier, and an amendment can turn a solicitation GPH already held into one that matches your profiles for the first time. Selecting a run date shows you everything that run found; expand it to narrow to a single posted date. See [Run date and posted date](/help/solicitation-matching#run-date-and-posted-date).

### Will I be notified about the same solicitation twice?

No. Once you've been matched on a solicitation, GPH won't surface it for you again on later runs, even if it reappears in a subsequent import.

### Who controls how often I get emails?

Each user sets their own alert frequency on the [Notifications](/help/notifications) page — an admin doesn't manage individual inboxes. The frequencies available to you depend on your plan: Free offers off or weekly, Basic adds daily, and Advanced adds immediate.

### What's the difference between the bell and email alerts?

They're independent. The in-app **notification bell** (🔔) shows new bid matches (and RFQ responses) without email; email **Bid Matching Alerts** land in your inbox on the schedule you choose. You can turn on either, both, or neither. See [The notification bell](/help/notifications#the-notification-bell-in-app-alerts) to configure the bell.

### Does bid matching cover SAM.gov as well as DIBBS?

Yes. GPH ingests solicitations from DLA, DIBBS, and SAM.gov. A single [Set-Aside condition](/help/bid-matching-profiles#condition-types) covers set-aside designations drawn from both DLA/DIBBS and SAM.gov sources, so you don't need a separate condition per source.

### What happens to my profiles if I downgrade or cancel my subscription?

They're deactivated, not deleted. Your configuration is preserved, and re-upgrading lets you turn profiles back on — you choose which ones. See [Setting Up Bid-Matching Profiles](/help/bid-matching-profiles) and [Plans and Pricing](/help/plans-and-pricing).

### If I cancel my paid plan, do I lose my account?

No. Ending a paid plan is a **downgrade to the Free tier**, not an account closure — you keep your account, your login, and your data (profiles beyond the Free limit are just paused). To actually stop using GPH entirely, an admin can **Close account** from the Billing page; that deactivates the account but still **doesn't delete your data**, and support can reopen it. See [Plans and Pricing](/help/plans-and-pricing#downgrading-to-free-ending-a-paid-plan).

### How do I add or remove seats?

Paid plans are billed per seat, and you manage seats yourself. Go to **Account \> Billing**, and on your plan card choose **Change seats** (up to 10). Stripe prorates the change on your next invoice. Every user on your account gets your full plan tier — seats set how many users you can have. To reduce seats below your current user count, deactivate users on the **Manage Users** page first. See [Plans and Pricing](/help/plans-and-pricing#adding-or-removing-seats).

### Why can I only add one user — even during my trial?

Your plan includes one seat per user, and you subscribed with one seat, so you can have one user until you add more seats. It's one seat per person, not a flat allowance. To add teammates, add seats first — on the **Manage Users** page, **+ Add User** will point you to the **Add a seat** shortcut. During a trial this costs nothing up front: set your seats to the number of people you want (up to 10), invite them, and you're billed for that count only when the trial converts.
