## Bid matching

### How quickly does a new profile start producing matches?

Matching runs once a day, after GPH imports new solicitations. A profile you create today is evaluated on the next run — usually within 24 hours. See [How Solicitation Matching Works](/help/solicitation-matching).

### Can I match against solicitations posted before I signed up?

No. Bid matching evaluates newly imported solicitations on each run, not the existing backlog. To research solicitations that are already posted, use the [Searching for Parts](/help/parts-search) and [Researching Vendors](/help/vendor-research) tools, which surface recent and open solicitations tied to a part or vendor.

### Will I be notified about the same solicitation twice?

No. Once you've been matched on a solicitation, GPH won't surface it for you again on later runs, even if it reappears in a subsequent import.

### Who controls how often I get emails?

Each user sets their own alert frequency on the [Notifications](/help/notifications) page — an admin doesn't manage individual inboxes. The frequencies available to you depend on your plan: Free offers off or weekly, Basic adds daily, and Advanced adds immediate.

### What's the difference between the bell and email alerts?

They're independent. The in-app **notification bell** (🔔) shows new bid matches (and RFQ responses) without email; email **Bid Matching Alerts** land in your inbox on the schedule you choose. You can turn on either, both, or neither. See [The notification bell](/help/notifications#the-notification-bell-in-app-alerts) to configure the bell.

### Does bid matching cover SAM.gov as well as DIBBS?

Yes. GPH ingests solicitations from DLA, DIBBS, and SAM.gov. A single [Set-Aside condition](/help/bid-matching-profiles#condition-types) covers set-aside designations drawn from both DLA/DIBBS and SAM.gov sources, so you don't need a separate condition per source.

### What happens to my profiles if I pause or cancel my subscription?

They're deactivated, not deleted. Your configuration is preserved, and reactivating your subscription lets you turn profiles back on — you choose which ones. See [Setting Up Bid-Matching Profiles](/help/bid-matching-profiles) and [Plans and Pricing](/help/plans-and-pricing).
