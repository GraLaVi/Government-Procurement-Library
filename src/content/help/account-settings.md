The Account Settings page is where you manage your individual profile, your password, your team, and the non-login contacts (such as billing or HR recipients) that receive emails on your organization's behalf. Click **Account** in the top-right of any page to open it.

## Profile Settings

The Profile Settings page is where you manage your individual information:

- **Profile photo** — Your initials are displayed as your avatar.  
- **First name** and **Last name** — Update at any time.  
- **Email address** — Displayed as read-only. To change your email, contact support.  
- **Account roles** — Your roles are shown for reference; they're assigned by your account admin from the Manage Users page.

Click **Save Changes** to save edits, or **Cancel** to discard them. Below the form, the **Account Information** block shows your Account ID, Customer ID, and email verification status — useful information for support inquiries.

## Changing your password

To change your password, go to **Account \> Change Password**. Enter your current password, then choose and confirm a new password.

GPH recommends a few password security practices:

- Use a unique password that you don't use for other accounts  
- Consider a password manager to generate and store strong passwords  
- Never share your password with anyone, including GPH support

If you've forgotten your password, log out and use the password reset link on the sign-in page. Admins can also reset another user's password from the Manage Users page.

## Preferences

The Preferences page lets you customize your experience inside GPH.

### Appearance

Choose how GPH appears in your browser:

- **Light** — Light theme  
- **Dark** — Dark theme  
- **System** — Match your device's system setting

### Default page

Pick where you'd like to land after signing in. The dashboard is used if no preference is set. Options include the **Dashboard**, **Parts search**, **Vendor search**, **Bid matching**, or **Account**.

### Results layout

Choose how vendor and part records are laid out when you open one:

- **Tabs** — Show one section at a time. Each section loads when you open its tab, so the view stays compact. This is the default.  
- **One page** — Stack every section on a single scrollable page with a jump-to navigation rail on the side. You see the whole record at once, and (on the Advanced plan) you can print or export the entire record together.

This single setting applies to both vendor profiles and part records. See [Researching vendors](/help/vendor-research) and [Searching for parts](/help/parts-search) for what each view contains.

### Cookie Preferences

GPH uses two categories of browser cookies:

- **Strictly necessary** — Required for sign-in and navigation. Always on.  
- **Functional** — Remembers preferences like theme. On by default; can be turned off.

For details on what data is stored in each category, see the cookie policy linked at the top of the section. Click **Manage cookie preferences** to change your selection.

## Managing users

The Manage Users page is where account admins add team members, change their roles, and remove users who've left the organization. The page shows your current users, their assigned roles, and the number of seats used against your plan's user capacity.

*Note: Manage Users is available to account admins only.*

**To add a user**, click **\+ Add User** and follow the prompts. New users receive an email invitation to set up their account.

**Per-user actions** are available from the actions menu (three-dot icon) at the end of each user row:

- **Edit User** — Update the user's profile information.  
- **Reset Password** — Trigger a password reset for the user. They'll receive an email with instructions.  
- **Deactivate** — Suspend the user's access without deleting their account. Their data is preserved, and you can reactivate them later. Toggle **Include inactive users** above the user table to see deactivated accounts.  
- **Delete User** — Remove the user permanently. This action can't be undone.

## Managing contacts

Contacts are non-login email recipients at your organization — people like your AP department, an HR distribution list, or a third-party billing recipient. They receive emails for the notification types they've been subscribed to, but they don't have accounts or access to the platform itself.

To add a contact, go to **Account \> Contacts** and click **\+ Add contact**. The contact form includes:

- **First name** and **Last name**  
- **Email**  
- **Type** — Choose from Accounting, HR, Billing, Operations, or Other. This is an organizational label for your own reference and has no effect on notifications or platform access.  
- **Job title** (optional)  
- **Phone** (optional)  
- **Primary contact** checkbox — A reference label for your own records. Designating a contact as primary has no effect on notifications or platform access.  
- 

Once added, contacts appear in your contacts list. To subscribe a contact to specific notification types (such as Bid Matching Alerts or System Announcements), go to the **Team Notifications** page.
