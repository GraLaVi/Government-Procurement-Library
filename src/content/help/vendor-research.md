Every government contractor has a CAGE code — a 5-character identifier assigned during SAM registration. GPH builds a complete profile for each vendor: their business details, SAM status, contacts, contracting history, and currently open solicitations. Use vendor research to understand your own market position, evaluate competitors, or vet potential partners.

## Where to search

GPH offers two entry points for vendor search.

**Dashboard quick search.** The **Look up a vendor by CAGE** bar at the top of your dashboard is the fastest way to research a vendor when you have their CAGE code. Enter the code and click **Go**.

**Library Search.** For broader options, click **Library Search** in the main navigation, then select the vendor search type. You can search by **CAGE Code**, **UEI** (Unique Entity Identifier), or **Entity Name**. Entity name search is useful when you know the company but not their CAGE.

## Reading a vendor profile

When you select a vendor from the search results, GPH opens their full profile. At the top, you'll see the vendor name with status badges: a **SAM status** badge (**Active**, **Inactive**, or **Expired**), an **Excluded** badge when the vendor is on the federal exclusion (debarment) list, and an **SB** badge when they qualify as a small business.

Below the header, the vendor's data is split across sections. Section counts in parentheses (e.g., "Recent Awards (50)") show how many records are available. How many sections you see depends on your plan.

## Tabs or one page

GPH can present a vendor profile two ways, and you choose which in **Account → Preferences** under **Results layout**:

- **Tabs** (the default) — Each section is its own tab, and you view one at a time. Sections load as you open them, keeping the page compact.  
- **One page** — Every section is stacked on a single scrollable page with a jump-to navigation rail, so you can scan the whole profile at once. On the Advanced plan, this view also lets you print or export the entire profile together.

The setting applies to both vendor profiles and part records, and you can switch back and forth at any time. The section descriptions below are the same regardless of which layout you choose. For more on changing preferences, see [Managing your account](/help/account-settings#results-layout).

## Demographics tab

The Demographics tab provides the vendor's basic identification and business details. A **hero card** at the top shows the company name (and any DBA) with the CAGE, UEI, small-business status, and SAM status inline. Below it are several cards:

- **Business Details.** Entity structure (corporation, LLC, etc.), website, state and country of incorporation, business type (e.g., Under 500 employee Corp), and the vendor's **DUNS** and **DoDAAC** identifiers.  
- **Registration.** SAM registration status (Active, Inactive, or Expired), exclusion status (shown as **Excluded** or **Not Excluded**), and **Reg. Expires** (the SAM registration expiration date).  
- **Certifications & Set-Asides.** When the vendor has published certifications, they appear here as chips, with set-aside designations tagged.  
- **Physical Address** and **Mailing Address.** The vendor's registered locations, including U.S. congressional district when applicable. The mailing address is shown only when it differs from the physical address.

The Demographics tab is your first stop when you need to confirm a vendor is currently eligible to receive federal contracts.

## Contacts tab

The Contacts tab lists the vendor's SAM-registered points of contact, organized by role. SAM defines several roles — **Government Business**, **Electronic Business**, and **Past Performance** — each with an **Alternate** variant. Each entry includes the person's name and role; some entries also include title, phone, fax, and email address.

These are the same contacts the government uses for official communications, which makes this tab valuable for outreach, partnership inquiries, or due diligence.

## Recent Awards tab

The Recent Awards tab lists contracts the vendor has received. Columns are **Award Date**, **Contract #**, **PR #** (purchase requisition), **NSN**, **Description**, **Qty**, **Unit Price**, **Total**, and **Agency**.

The **NSN** column links to the part's full profile — making it possible to drill from a vendor into the specific parts they've supplied — and the **Contract #** cell has a button to open the award document as a PDF. To learn more about viewing contract documents, see [Viewing solicitations and contracts](/help/solicitations-and-contracts).

## Contracts Booked tab

The Contracts Booked tab is one of GPH's most powerful competitive intelligence views. It shows a 13-month rolling summary of the vendor's contract volume, broken down by DLA distribution center.

At the top of the tab, a **13-Month Summary** card shows the vendor's total contracted value across the past 13 months, segmented into columns **DSCP**, **DSCR**, **DSCC**, **Other**, and **Grand Total**. The distribution-center codes stand for:

- **DSCP** — Defense Supply Center, Philadelphia  
- **DSCR** — Defense Supply Center, Richmond  
- **DSCC** — Defense Supply Center, Columbus  
- **Other** — Other agencies and procurement sources

Below the summary, a month-by-month table shows columns **Month**, **DSCP**, **DSCR**, **DSCC**, **Other**, and **Total**. For each month and each center, GPH displays both the dollar value contracted **and** the vendor's **Rank** among all vendors at that center for that month.

The ranking data is unique. A vendor ranked \#4 at DSCC for a given month was the fourth-largest contract recipient at that center that month. Use the rank data to:

- Identify how your own organization ranks compared to competitors at each distribution center  
- Spot centers where competitors are strong but you're absent  
- Understand seasonality in a vendor's contracting volume

## Open Solicitations tab

The Open Solicitations tab shows currently active solicitations relevant to the vendor's product lines and contracting history. Columns are **Close Date**, **Solicitation #**, **NSN**, **Description**, **Qty**, **Est. Value**, **Set-Aside** (a code badge; hover for the full label), and **Agency**.

Rows can come from DLA/DIBBS or SAM.gov. Changed solicitations show an amber **Amended** badge you can click for a change timeline. SAM.gov opportunities add a color-coded notice-type chip (Solicitation, Combined, Presolicitation, Sources Sought, or Opportunity), a button to open attached SAM documents, and — when an opportunity matches several parts — a chip showing the number of matched NSNs. To learn more about viewing a solicitation PDF or amendment history, see [Viewing solicitations and contracts](/help/solicitations-and-contracts).

Look up a competitor's CAGE code and open this tab to see what they're positioned to bid on right now.

*Note: this tab is available on the Advanced plan.*

## Exporting data

The data tabs — Recent Awards, Contracts Booked, and Open Solicitations — include an **Export CSV** button that downloads the visible data to a spreadsheet. This is especially useful for Recent Awards (often dozens to hundreds of rows) and Contracts Booked (where you may want to perform additional analysis on rank and dollar volume data).

For more detailed or custom exports, click **Request a custom report →** at the bottom of any data table.

*Note: CSV export is available on the Advanced plan.*
