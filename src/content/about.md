title: "About Government Procurement Hub"
---

Government Procurement Hub is a federal procurement intelligence platform built for the businesses that make up the government supply chain — manufacturers, distributors, and resellers selling parts and commodities to federal and defense agencies.

We aggregate solicitations from procurement channels including SAM.gov and DLA/DIBBS and match them against your product catalog and part numbers. Whether you're monitoring for new bids, tracking competitor awards, or looking for supply gaps to fill, you see what's relevant to your business without manually searching dozens of sources.

Most suppliers losing government contracts aren't losing on qualifications. They never saw the solicitation. That's the problem we built GPH to solve.

The platform is backed by over 15 years of direct experience building procurement tools for the defense supply chain — and by a team that believes this market deserves modern, reliable software with transparent pricing and self-serve onboarding. No demo required to see what it costs. No sales call required to get started.
