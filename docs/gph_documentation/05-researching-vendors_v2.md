Every government contractor has a CAGE code — a 5-character identifier assigned during SAM registration. GPH builds a complete profile for each vendor: their business details, SAM status, contacts, contracting history, and currently open solicitations. Use vendor research to understand your own market position, evaluate competitors, or vet potential partners.

## Where to search

GPH offers two entry points for vendor search.

**Dashboard quick search.** The **Look up a vendor by CAGE** bar at the top of your dashboard is the fastest way to research a vendor when you have their CAGE code. Enter the code and click **Go**.

**Library Search.** For broader options, click **Library Search** in the main navigation, then select the vendor search type. You can search by CAGE code, entity name, or UEI (Unique Entity Identifier). Entity name search is useful when you know the company but not their CAGE.

## Reading a vendor profile

When you select a vendor from the search results, GPH opens their full profile. At the top, you'll see the vendor name with status badges indicating whether they're currently active in SAM and whether they qualify for small business set-asides (an **SB** badge appears when applicable).

Below the header, five tabs cover different aspects of the vendor. Tab counts in parentheses (e.g., "Recent Awards (50)") show how many records are available.

## Demographics tab

The Demographics tab provides the vendor's basic identification and business details, organized into cards:

- **Identifiers.** CAGE code and UEI (Unique Entity Identifier).  
- **Business Details.** Entity structure (corporation, LLC, etc.), website, state and country of incorporation, and business type (e.g., Under 500 employee Corp).  
- **Registration.** SAM registration status (Active, Inactive, or Expired), exclusion status (whether the vendor is on the federal debarment list), and SAM registration expiration date.  
- **Physical Address** and **Mailing Address.** The vendor's registered locations, including U.S. congressional district when applicable.

The Demographics tab is your first stop when you need to confirm a vendor is currently eligible to receive federal contracts.

## Contacts tab

The Contacts tab lists the vendor's SAM-registered points of contact, organized by role. SAM defines several roles — Government Business, Government, Electronic, and Past Performance — each with an Alternate (Alt) variant. Each entry includes the person's name and role; some entries also include title, phone number, and email address.

These are the same contacts the government uses for official communications, which makes this tab valuable for outreach, partnership inquiries, or due diligence.

## Recent Awards tab

The Recent Awards tab lists contracts the vendor has received, with award date, contract number, purchase requisition number, NSN, part description, quantity, unit price, total contract value, and source agency.

NSNs link to the part's full profile — making it possible to drill from a vendor into the specific parts they've supplied. To view a contract document or amendment history, see [Viewing solicitations and contracts](http:///help/solicitations-and-contracts).

## Contracts Booked tab

The Contracts Booked tab is one of GPH's most powerful competitive intelligence views. It shows a 13-month rolling summary of the vendor's contract volume, broken down by DLA distribution center.

At the top of the tab, a summary row shows the vendor's total contracted value across the past 13 months, segmented by:

- **DSCP** — Defense Supply Center, Philadelphia  
- **DSCR** — Defense Supply Center, Richmond  
- **DSCC** — Defense Supply Center, Columbus  
- **OTHER** — Other agencies and procurement sources  
- **GRAND TOTAL** — Combined total across all sources

Below the summary, a month-by-month breakdown shows the same columns. For each month and each center, GPH displays both the dollar value contracted **and** the vendor's rank among all vendors at that center for that month.

The ranking data is unique. A vendor ranked \#4 at DSCC for a given month was the fourth-largest contract recipient at that center that month. Use the rank data to:

- Identify how your own organization ranks compared to competitors at each distribution center  
- Spot centers where competitors are strong but you're absent  
- Understand seasonality in a vendor's contracting volume

## Open Solicitations tab

The Open Solicitations tab shows currently active solicitations relevant to the vendor's product lines and contracting history. Columns include close date, solicitation number, NSN, description, quantity, estimated value, set-aside type, and agency. To view a solicitation PDF or amendment history, see [Viewing solicitations and contracts](http:///help/solicitations-and-contracts).

Look up a competitor's CAGE code and open this tab to see what they're positioned to bid on right now.

*Note: this tab is available on the Advanced plan.*

## Exporting data

Most vendor profile tabs include an **Export CSV** button that downloads the visible data to a spreadsheet. This is especially useful for Recent Awards (often dozens to hundreds of rows) and Contracts Booked (where you may want to perform additional analysis on rank and dollar volume data).

For more detailed or custom exports, click **Request a custom report →** at the bottom of any data table.

*Note: CSV export is available on the Advanced plan.*
