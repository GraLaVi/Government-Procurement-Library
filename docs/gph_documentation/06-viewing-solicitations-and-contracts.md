## **URL:** `/help/solicitations-and-contracts` **Page title:** Viewing Solicitations and Contracts | GPH Help **Meta description:** Learn how to view solicitation and contract PDFs and amendment history in Government Procurement Hub. **H1:** Viewing solicitations and contracts

Solicitations and contracts in GPH are available as the actual government documents — Requests for Quotation (RFQs), purchase orders, and other official PDFs — viewable directly inside the platform.

This article explains how to view solicitation and contract PDFs, how to interpret amendment history, and where the relevant controls appear.

## Three actions on a solicitation or contract row

Anywhere a solicitation or contract appears in GPH, you'll see three interactive elements:

- **The number itself (e.g., `SPE7L3-26-T-2086`)** — Clicking the number runs a filtered search for that solicitation or contract, useful for finding all related parts.  
- **The doc icon (next to the number)** — Clicking the icon opens the government PDF in an in-app viewer.  
- **The "Amended" badge (when present)** — Clicking the badge opens an amendment history modal.

## Opening a PDF

Click the doc icon next to any solicitation or contract number. GPH opens the document in a modal PDF viewer where you can:

- Navigate through pages using the thumbnails on the left or the pagination controls at the top  
- Zoom in and out  
- Search within the document  
- Download or print the PDF  
- Click **Open in new tab** to view the document in a standalone browser tab

The viewer displays the full government form — for solicitations, typically a Request for Quotation; for contracts, an Order for Supplies or Services or other DD-form document. These are the same documents you'd receive directly from DLA, DIBBS, or other procurement systems.

## Viewing amendment history

When a solicitation has been revised since posting, an **Amended** badge appears next to its number. Click the badge to open the Amendment history modal, which shows each change as a separate entry. Each entry includes:

- The change number (e.g., "Amendment 1")  
- A summary of what changed (e.g., "Status changed")  
- A timestamp showing when the change was recorded  
- The source of the change (e.g., "VIA DIBBS")  
- Before and after values, with the old value struck through and the new value following

Review amendments carefully. Amendments can change quantities, requirements, set-aside types, or close dates — acting on outdated information could mean preparing a non-conforming bid.

## Where to find solicitations and contracts

Doc icons and "Amended" badges appear consistently throughout GPH wherever solicitations or contracts are listed:

- **Bid-Matching results** — In your match history, every matched solicitation has a doc icon and amendment badge (if applicable).  
- **Part records** — On the Recent Solicitations and Procurement History tabs ([Searching for Parts](http:///help/parts-search)).  
- **Vendor profiles** — On the Open Solicitations and Recent Awards tabs ([Researching Vendors](http:///help/vendor-research)).
